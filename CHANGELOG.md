# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- **One finish for the whole family.** Choosing OBSIDIAN or AURORA here switches every Paragon app, open ones included (within a couple of seconds), and every app starts in the family's choice.
- In AURORA, group titles and selected rows are dark and readable (they were white on the light finish).

## 0.2.1

- Knows the whole family: PR7-DU, PR7-CB, PR7-IS, PR7-PM and PR7-SM are in its list of family apps (their permanent install IDs), so the family links find them when they're installed.

## 0.2.0

- **Check for Updates knows PR7-U**, the family installer. When it's installed, the update window has **Update All in PR7-U**, and a check by hand that finds PR7-R up to date offers to open PR7-U for the rest of the family.

## 0.1.0

- First version: a PDF reader that works the way SumatraPDF works, in the Paragon family look.
- Tabs (more PDFs opened from Explorer join the open window), the start page with Frequently read, and every
  document reopening where it was left (page, zoom, layout, rotation); the last session comes back at start.
- Single page, facing and book view, continuous or a spread at a time; rotation; presentation (F5) and fullscreen (F11).
- Fit page, fit width, actual size, Sumatra's zoom steps and custom zoom; Ctrl+wheel zooms at the mouse.
- Bookmarks panel; Find with Match case and the matches marked on the pages; links with Back / Forward.
- Select and copy text, favorites, recent files, Go to page, Print, Properties, Save As, Rename, Show in folder.
- Password-protected PDFs; a file changed on disk is reloaded at the same page.
- Registers as a PDF reader (Open with, Default apps); Check for updates; OBSIDIAN and AURORA finishes.
